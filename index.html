How it works

Simple and upfront

No surprise bills, no pushy quotes. Four steps, and you only pay if you're happy.

1

Get in touch

Call or email us. Tell us roughly what needs doing.

2

We pop round

We'll come out at a time that suits you, with our own kit.

3

£30 base fee

Covers our time and travel for up to two hours of work.

4

Two-hour guarantee

If a job takes longer than two hours, we refund your £30. Our promise.

The two-hour guarantee

It's our way of saying: pay us if we got the job done quickly and well. If it takes longer than expected, that's on us, not you. We want this to feel fair — and to build a name in the neighbourhood for honest work.
